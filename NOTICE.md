# Project Lineage & Attribution Notice

**The Bible Maker** (`https://github.com/SalitaNgDiyos/The-Bible-Maker`) is an
independent offshoot maintained by **Edmund P. Valdez (`SalitaNgDiyos`)**.

- It is **not** a continuation of, nor officially endorsed by or affiliated with,
  upstream `rubiot/ibiblia`.
- It originally branched from **iBiblia 1.1.0** by **Rúbio R. C. Terra**
  (`https://github.com/rubiot/ibiblia`) and has diverged with a 64-bit port,
  native Linux GTK2 port, theming engine, interactive Bible tree & USFM 3.0 /
  USFX / SWORD workflows, Find & Replace, Change Log, Crash Tracker, and direct
  GitHub & Timotheos live publishing.
- Issues, feature requests, and releases specific to **The Bible Maker** belong
  at: `https://github.com/SalitaNgDiyos/The-Bible-Maker/issues`
